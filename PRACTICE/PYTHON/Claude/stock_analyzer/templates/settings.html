Scoring System
Formula Generală:

Total Score = (Sentiment × 35%) + (Technical × 40%) + (Fundamental × 25%)

Sentiment Score (35%)
  • News sentiment: 40%
  • Social media: 30%
  • Market correlation: 30%
Technical Score (40%)
  • RSI: 25%
  • MACD: 25%
  • Moving Averages: 30%
  • Volume: 20%
Fundamental Score (25%)
  • P/E Ratio: 30%
  • EPS Growth: 25%
  • Revenue Growth: 25%
  • Debt-to-Equity: 20%
Clasificare Sentiment:
Score Range Classification Recommendation
75 - 100 Strong Bullish BUY
60 - 75 Bullish BUY
40 - 60 Neutral HOLD
25 - 40 Bearish SELL
0 - 25 Strong Bearish SELL
Price Target & Stop Loss
Target Price:

Target Price = Current Price × (1 + Expected Return)
Expected Return = (Technical Score + Fundamental Score) / 200 × Risk Multiplier

Stop Loss:

Stop Loss = Current Price × (1 - Risk Factor)
Risk Factor = (100 - Total Score) / 100 × 0.15

* Bonus pentru sentiment foarte bullish (Score > 75): +20% la expected return
* Minimum stop loss: 5% din prețul curent
